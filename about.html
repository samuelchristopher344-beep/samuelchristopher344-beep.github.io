About christoflightX-1

Engineering ideas, experiments, and future systems built from curiosity and creativity.

christoflightX-1 is an engineering innovation platform created to explore ideas, develop new concepts, and document the process of turning imagination into practical solutions.

The goal is simple: combine curiosity with engineering discipline. From drones and propulsion systems to future transportation concepts, each project represents a new opportunity to learn, design, test, improve, and share knowledge.

This portfolio is a place for experimenting with technology, studying real engineering challenges, and building a stronger understanding of how future systems may work in the real world.
