Drone Prototype — First Build

In build

My first drone project: the main body structure is complete, and I am moving into electronics, balance tuning, and future flight testing.

Project status

This drone prototype is my first serious attempt at designing and building a flying machine. The main frame and structure are complete. The next phase is installing electronics, checking balance, and preparing for flight testing.

The airframe is a quadcopter built from PVC pipe arms, a central hub, and 3D-printed motor mounts. It is a learning platform first: simple, repairable, and easy to change as I learn what works.

Current focus

  • Prototype drone frame construction
  • Structural balance and stability analysis
  • Electronic component layout planning
  • Future flight testing and tuning

Design goals

  • Lightweight frame
  • Modular component mounting
  • Simple and practical prototype layout
  • Learning platform for future improvement

Build progress

  • Current stage — Main structure completed.
  • Next step — Add motors and electronics.
  • Future work — Balance tuning and first test flights.

What I am learning

A first airframe teaches geometry before it teaches flight. Arm length, motor spacing, and how the center hub holds everything together decide whether the craft can be balanced at all. The 3D-printed mounts let me iterate on motor attachment without rebuilding the whole frame.

When electronics go in, the next problems are mass distribution, cable routing, and keeping the flight controller isolated from vibration. Those are the questions this prototype exists to answer.
