Transportation

Future Mobility Systems

Concept

Investigating new transportation concepts that blend sustainability, efficiency, and engineering innovation for future mobility solutions.

The question

Future mobility is not one vehicle. It is a set of systems that move people or payloads with less waste and more control — on water, in the air, and on the ground.

This page collects the transportation ideas that sit above the individual prototypes: how a drone, a watercraft, and a ground vehicle could share structure, propulsion thinking, and control methods.

Design principles

  • Keep structures light without making them fragile
  • Use electric drive where control and packaging matter most
  • Design for repair — a prototype that cannot be opened will not be improved
  • Test small before committing to a full-size machine

From concept to system

A mobility concept only becomes engineering when it has a mass budget, a propulsion path, and a way to fail safely. The drone is the first of those systems in physical form. The watercraft is the marine counterpart. Ground mobility is still on paper.

The long-term aim is a family of machines that share a way of thinking, not a single product.
