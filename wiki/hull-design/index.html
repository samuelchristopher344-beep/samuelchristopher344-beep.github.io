Small High-Speed Hulls

Concept

The basic geometry of a compact planing hull and why intake placement is part of the hull, not an accessory.

Short hulls are strict

A long displacement hull can hide a lot of mistakes. A short high-speed hull cannot. The entry has to cut, the aft has to plane, and the intake for a water-jet has to stay wet.

Three surfaces

  • Entry — how the hull meets oncoming water
  • Planing flats — how it rises and sheds drag
  • Intake and tunnel — how water reaches the pump

How I will test it

A small model in a pool answers more than a render. Does it sit level? Does the bow rise cleanly? Does the intake breathe air in a turn? Those are hull questions, and they come before motors.
