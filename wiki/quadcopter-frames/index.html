Quadcopter Frames

Research

How arm length, hub layout, and motor spacing decide whether a first drone can be balanced and repaired.

The job of a frame

A quadcopter frame holds four motors in a plane, keeps them aligned, and gives the electronics a place to sit. Everything else — looks, extra arms, decoration — is secondary.

On a first build, the frame should be easy to measure, easy to replace, and stiff enough that motor thrust does not twist the arms.

Geometry that matters

  • Motor-to-motor distance, which sets propeller clearance
  • Arm stiffness, which sets how much the motors tilt under load
  • Hub size, which sets where the battery and controller can sit
  • Landing clearance, so props do not hit the ground on touchdown

On this build

The christoflightX-1 prototype uses PVC arms and printed motor mounts. That choice trades peak stiffness for speed of making. It is the right trade for a first airframe, and the wrong trade for a final one. The wiki exists to remember that distinction.
