Control Loops on Small Machines

Research

A plain-language look at sensing, deciding, and actuating — the loop a drone cannot fly without.

Sense, decide, act

A control loop reads a sensor, compares that reading to a target, and moves an actuator. On a quadcopter the sensors are gyros and accelerometers, the target is a level or commanded attitude, and the actuators are four motors.

If the loop is slow, the craft wobbles. If the sensors are noisy, the motors twitch. If the frame is flexible, the loop fights a moving target.

Why the frame comes first

You cannot tune a controller on a structure that bends in unknown ways. That is why this project finished the airframe before the flight controller. The mechanical loop has to be boring before the electronic loop can be interesting.
