Smart Systems

Intelligent Mechanical Systems

Research

Combining automation and sensor-based control to improve system efficiency, responsiveness, and usefulness in real-world engineering applications.

The idea

A smart mechanical system is a machine that can sense its own state and adjust. Sensors, control logic, and actuators close a loop that a purely mechanical device cannot close on its own.

On a drone, that loop is already required: a flight controller reads gyros and drives motors many times a second. That is the first smart system in this portfolio, even if the airframe is still a PVC prototype.

Where it applies

  • Flight stabilization and attitude control
  • Balance and vibration sensing on prototypes
  • Throttle and directional control on marine craft
  • Safety cutoffs when a sensor reading leaves a safe range

Working method

I start with the mechanism, then add only the sensing that the mechanism actually needs. Extra sensors without a control purpose become noise. The drone will teach that lesson in hardware: which measurements change flight, and which ones only look impressive on a diagram.
