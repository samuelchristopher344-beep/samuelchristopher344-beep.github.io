Problem-Solving Designs

Research

Turning real challenges into practical concept designs using creative engineering reasoning, efficiency analysis, and iterative improvement.

Start from the constraint

A useful design begins with a limit: mass, cost, workshop tools, or a material that is already on the bench. The drone started with a first-build constraint — I needed a frame I could actually make. That constraint shaped the whole layout.

Problem-solving on this site means writing the constraint down, listing the failed options, and keeping the version that survives contact with the real world.

A working loop

  • Name the problem in one sentence
  • Sketch two or three mechanical answers
  • Build the cheapest test that can kill a bad idea
  • Keep notes on what broke and why

Why document failures

A prototype that fails and is not written down teaches once. A prototype that fails and is recorded teaches the next project. That is the reason this platform exists.
