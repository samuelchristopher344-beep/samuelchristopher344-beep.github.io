Mechanical Innovation

Advanced Mechanisms

Research

Designing efficient mechanical arrangements that improve strength, movement, and reliability while still staying lightweight and practical.

Mechanism first

Before electronics, a machine is joints, load paths, and materials. The drone frame is a mechanism: four arms, a hub, and mounts that have to keep motors aligned under vibration.

Advanced here does not mean complicated. It means choosing an arrangement that carries load cleanly and can be rebuilt when it fails.

What I look for

  • Short, direct load paths from motor to hub
  • Joints that can be tightened, inspected, and replaced
  • Mass kept close to the center unless the layout needs otherwise
  • Printed parts used where geometry is hard to make by hand

On the current frame

PVC is not a flight-grade carbon spar. It is an honest first material: cheap, cuttable, and clear when it flexes. The 3D-printed mounts take over where the pipe cannot hold a motor cleanly. That split — simple structure, specific printed interfaces — is the mechanism idea I want to reuse.
