Exploring emerging ideas and future technologies that can reshape transportation, manufacturing, energy systems, and industrial design.

A place for unfinished ideas

Not every idea is ready to become a prototype. This page is for the ones that are still questions: new vehicle layouts, unusual propulsion paths, and manufacturing methods that could make small prototypes stronger or lighter.

The rule is that a concept has to name a problem. A drawing without a problem is decoration.

Active threads

How a concept graduates

An idea moves off this page when it has a first physical test: a frame, a hull model, a motor rig. The drone already made that jump. The watercraft is next in line.
