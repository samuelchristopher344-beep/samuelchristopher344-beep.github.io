Prototype Testing

Testing

Testing prototypes to validate design assumptions, study weaknesses, and understand how systems move, behave, and respond under real conditions.

What testing is for

A prototype is a question in physical form. Testing is how the question gets an answer. The goal is not to prove the design is already right. The goal is to find the first thing that is wrong.

For the drone, early tests will be static: does the frame flex, do the mounts hold, is the mass roughly centered. Flight comes after those answers.

Test types I use

  • Fit and alignment checks before power is applied
  • Mass and balance measurements
  • No-load motor spin tests, away from people
  • Tethered or limited-range trials before free flight

Safety

Rotors and high-speed hulls are not toys. Tests are planned, limited, and stopped when a result is unclear. A crashed prototype that nobody can explain is not data.
