Research

Evaluating materials, systems, and new engineering methods to better understand what works and why.

Research as workshop work

Research here is not a literature review for its own sake. It is asking why a material, a joint, or a propulsion layout belongs on a prototype I can actually build.

PVC, printed polymer mounts, and later carbon or aluminum will all be judged the same way: mass, stiffness, repairability, and cost.

Current topics

  • Frame materials for small drones
  • 3D-printed interfaces between standard parts
  • Water-jet versus propeller for small hulls
  • Control methods that a first builder can actually tune
